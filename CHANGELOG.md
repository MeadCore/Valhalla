# What's new in Valhalla

## 2.2.0 (November 23, 2026)

- **Your own themes.** Make a look in [Asgard Theme Studio](https://github.com/MeadCore/Asgard-Theme-Studio) (free, no code), save it, then tap the scene button › **Import theme…**. It shows up under **Your themes** with colours, button and label styles, fonts, textures, a moving background and your own picture. Its colours also paint the color lights like a scene. Themes are checked before use and never contain code or links.

## 2.1.0 (November 22, 2026)

- **Phone PIN.** Phones and tablets that open Valhalla over Tailscale now have to type a PIN (4 to 8 digits) once. After that the phone is remembered. Five wrong tries lock PIN entry for 5 minutes. The wall-screen PC itself never needs the PIN. Set it in ⚙ Setup → Phones & board. Valhalla won't turn on phone access until a PIN is set.
- **Forgot the PIN?** On the wall-screen PC, type a new one in ⚙ Setup → Phones & board. That also signs every phone out. There's a "Sign out all phones" button too.
- **Check for updates.** ❔ Help → About & updates shows your version and asks GitHub if there's a newer one. It can also check by itself (off unless you turn it on, at most twice a day). It never downloads or installs anything.
- **Wall-screen (kiosk) mode no longer uses Microsoft Edge.** It opens in Brave if you have it, otherwise Google Chrome, otherwise your normal browser.
- **Credits.** About now shows "Created October 26, 2026 by The_Mead_Maker & Odin". Valhalla is MIT licensed (see LICENSE).

## 2.0.0 (November 20, 2026)

- Govee lights: on/off, dimming, colors, whites and Govee's own scenes, over your home Wi-Fi (LAN) or Govee's cloud.
- Holiday scene colors spread across your Govee lights. All off, Away and Night switch them off.
- Setup wizard step for the Govee API key, and a plain-language Govee guide.
- Valhalla.exe and an installer, like Asgard.

## 1.0 (October 26, 2026)

- The first Valhalla: cameras, thermostats, garage, lights, calendar, the shared dry-erase board, holiday scenes and the Setup wizard, running on pretend devices.
